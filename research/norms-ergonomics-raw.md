## Standards checked but no public numeric value found (paywalled / scope only)

- **EN 14688:2015+A1:2018 "Sanitary appliances — Wash basins — Functional requirements and test methods"** (adopted in Ukraine as ДСТУ EN 14688:2019, IDT). Scope (per catalog abstract pages, e.g. iteh.ai, globalspec, bsigroup): specifies functional characteristics and test methods for wash basins "for domestic purposes," where "domestic purposes" also covers hotels, student accommodation, hospitals and similar buildings (except where special medical provisions apply); appliance shape is left to manufacturer discretion, drawings in the standard are examples only. Full text paywalled (iteh.ai, bsigroup, ansi webstore); no public numeric value found for bowl depth, overflow, splash, or dimensional tolerances. Confirmed to exist as a Ukrainian national standard (ДСТУ EN 14688:2019) per budstandart.com catalog listing, also full-text-inaccessible without purchase.
  - https://standards.iteh.ai/catalog/standards/cen/c086d3de-2e00-44bf-b1b5-4f8a7161e7eb/en-14688-2015
  - https://online.budstandart.com/ua/catalog/doc-page?id_doc=88386 (ДСТУ EN 14688:2019 listing)
- **EN 200 "Sanitary tapware — Single taps and combination taps for water supply systems of type 1 and type 2 — General technical specification."** Scope confirmed via search-result abstracts; full text paywalled (en-standard.eu / bsi). No public numeric value found (flow rate/pressure specs exist inside but were not accessible).
- **EN 817 "Sanitary tapware — Mechanical mixing valves (PN 10) — General technical specifications."** Scope: covers dimensional, leak-tightness, pressure-resistance, hydraulic-performance, mechanical-strength, endurance, corrosion-resistance, test-sequence and acoustic requirements for mechanical mixing valves used with sanitary appliances in personal-hygiene rooms (bathrooms, cloakrooms, etc). Full text paywalled; no public numeric value found.
  - https://www.en-standard.eu/din-en-817-sanitary-tapware-mechanical-mixing-valves-pn-10-general-technical-specifications/
- **EN 274 "Waste fittings for sanitary appliances — Requirements."** Scope confirmed (waste/drain fitting requirements); full text paywalled; no public numeric value found relevant to basin/faucet geometric compatibility.
- **ISO** — no ISO standard specifically governing wash-basin or bathroom-faucet installation dimensions/ergonomics was found. In the European context this space is covered by EN standards (EN 14688 for basins, EN 200/817 for tapware) rather than ISO; searches for "ISO wash basin dimensions" returned only commercial/retail content, not a relevant ISO standard. Treat as a confirmed gap rather than an unresearched one.
- **DIN 18040-2 "Barrierefreies Bauen — Öffentlich zugängliche Gebäude und Arbeitsstätten — Teil 2: Wohnungen"** (and DIN 18040-1 for public buildings) — the primary standard text is paywalled at din.de / beuth.de. All DIN 18040-2 numeric values in the table above were sourced from secondary German professional/consumer-advice websites (din18040.de, nullbarriere.de, baunetzwissen.de, aktion-barrierefreies-bad.de) that quote the standard's figures; these sources partially disagree with each other on two values (washbasin height and faucet-to-front-edge distance — see Conflicts). No independent primary-text confirmation was obtained.

### UA (Ukraine)
- No ДБН (or any UA-jurisdiction) clause found giving a general (non-accessible) recommended horizontal distance from faucet outlet/aerator to the rear edge of the basin, or a "water landing point" / target zone within the bowl. ## Conflicts

1. **Accessible washbasin installation height — UA, two ДБН documents disagree.**
   - ДБН В.2.5-64:2012 "Внутрішній водопровід та каналізація" (Таблиця 24) gives **500 mm** to top of rim for a combined column "у дошкільних закладах і в приміщеннях для інвалідів, які пересуваються за допомогою різних пристроїв" (preschools AND rooms for disabled people using mobility devices).
   - ДБН В.2.2-40:2018 (зі Зміною №3, чинна з 01.05.2025) "Інклюзивність будівель і споруд", п. 11.6.3, gives **750–850 mm** for the accessible/universal washbasin, explicitly with no pedestal/support underneath so a wheelchair can roll under it.
   - These are contradictory: 500 mm is far too low for wheelchair knee/leg clearance underneath the basin (which typically needs the rim near 800+ mm to leave ~700 mm clear height beneath). ДБН В.2.5-64's table appears to be an unrevised carry-over from the older Soviet-era SNiP 2.04.01-85 table and pre-dates the inclusive-design norm; ДБН В.2.2-40 (2018, amended 2025) is the newer, purpose-built accessibility standard and is the more credible source for wheelchair-accessible basin height. The 500 mm value in ДБН В.2.5-64 may in practice be intended more for the "дошкільні заклади" (preschool/children) half of that same combined column than for adult wheelchair users, but the table does not separate the two populations.
   - Recommendation for the app: treat 750–850 mm (ДБН В.2.2-40) as the accessible/universal-design value, and flag 500 mm (ДБН В.2.5-64) separately as a legacy/children-oriented value rather than merging them.

2. **DIN 18040-2 accessible washbasin height and faucet distance — secondary sources disagree with each other.**
   - Several independent German summary sites (din18040.de, nullbarriere.de) state the washbasin front-edge height must be **≤80 cm**, while the "Aktion Barrierefreies Bad" checklist PDF states **80–85 cm**.
   - Faucet distance from the front edge is given as **≤40 cm** by din18040.de/nullbarriere.de/bau-szene.de, but the Aktion Barrierefreies Bad checklist instead states "at least 20 cm" (a minimum, not a maximum, and a different number).
   - The DIN 18040-2 primary text itself is paywalled (din.de / beuth.de), so this could not be resolved against the authoritative source. Both readings are plausible transcription/summary variants of the same clause; the app should treat the DIN-18040-2-derived numbers as indicative only, not verified against primary text, and prefer the multiply-corroborated values (≤80 cm height, ≤40 cm faucet distance, ≥55 cm knee clearance) as the more likely-correct ones since they are corroborated by 2-3 independent secondary sources vs. 1 for the alternates.

- **UA (Ukraine) — strongest coverage, as intended.** Read full primary text (via downloaded PDFs + pdftotext, not just search snippets) of ДБН В.2.5-64:2012 (Таблиця 24: mandatory mounting heights for washbasins/sinks/baths/urinals/showers for 3 building-use categories, plus faucet-above-rim heights, axis spacing, side clearances) and ДБН В.2.2-40:2018 as amended by Зміна №3 (accessible washbasin height, no-pedestal rule, faucet-to-front-edge distance, faucet type, mirror heights, general control-height rule, children's washbasin height, room/aisle dimensions). ~24 UA rows. - **EU/EN — weak, as expected.** Identified and scoped EN 14688 (wash basins), EN 200/817 (tapware), EN 274 (waste fittings) — all exist and are correctly scoped, but all are paywalled with zero public numeric content found. No EN rows in the data table; all four listed under paywalled-standards.
- **ISO — confirmed empty.** No relevant ISO standard exists for this domain; not a research gap, a confirmed absence.
- **DE/DIN — moderate, but caveated.** DIN 18040-2 numeric values obtained only via secondary/professional summary sites (primary text paywalled), with two internal conflicts between those secondary sources (basin height 80cm vs 80–85cm; faucet distance ≤40cm vs ≥20cm). ~11 DE rows, all flagged as secondary-sourced.
